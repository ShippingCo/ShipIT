Start here

Use ShippingCo to book parcels, organise delivery work and keep clear money records.

How to use this guide

  • Start with the table of contents. Select a feature name to open its chapter.
  • Read the purpose and the steps beside the screenshots. Button names match the web app.
  • Screenshots use example test records. Names, dates, amounts and balances in them are not your business records.
  • Some screenshots show an empty page. This means no matching records were found, or setup is still needed. Follow the chapter’s first action.
  • The options you see depend on your role and selected branch. Ask your administrator for access if a needed option is missing.
  • A booking, a money receipt and a delivery confirmation are different records. Saving one does not automatically complete the others.

First-time setup

  • Sign in with the email registered for your business. If you have no workspace, enter your business name and first location name.
  • Choose the correct branch in the Franchise selector at the top. Your work is saved for that branch.
  • Ask the owner or branch administrator to set up active prices and tax rules in Settings & help. Both must cover the booking time.
  • Set up receiving accounts before recording money. Set up cash locations if your team uses cash drawers or agent cash custody.
  • Create customer accounts only for customers who have agreed monthly billing. A normal one-off booking does not need a monthly account.
  • Invite staff and give each person only the role needed for their work. A shared password is not needed for email-code sign-in.

A normal day at the counter

  • Create a booking: choose the sender, enter the receiver, add parcels, select service and check the bill.
  • Print the saved booking receipt when needed. Record money received as a separate action.
  • Use Packages to find a parcel by its tracking number. Use parcel groups and routes to prepare dispatch work.
  • Record handovers, departure, delivery attempts and delivery proof in the correct delivery screens.
  • Record expenses, cash handovers and any approved refunds when they happen.
  • At the end of the day, review outstanding money, count each cash drawer and use the closing flow.
  • Open reports to review saved business figures. A report does not move money or change a parcel’s status.

Sidebar and common controls

  • Create: start New booking, Record received money or Arrange a pickup, when your role allows it.
  • Overview: see the day’s figures and work that needs attention.
  • Bookings: open the parcel list, New Booking and Receipts.
  • Delivery: work with pickups, parcel groups, routes, handovers, delivery work and e-way records.
  • Money: record receipts, manage cash, customer accounts, agent money, approvals and bank checking.
  • Customers: find a saved sender and reuse their details.
  • Reports: choose the report that answers your business question.
  • Settings & help: change business setup and open messages or human support.
  • On a narrow screen, open the menu button to see the sidebar. Wide tables may scroll sideways; some lists become stacked rows.
  • Search and filters change which records are shown. Load more brings the next page of results; the first page may not contain every record.

Understand the result of an action

  • Loading means the app is still checking. Wait before trying the action again.
  • A saved or confirmed message means the app received a confirmed result.
  • An uncertain result means the connection stopped before the result was confirmed. Use the matching Retry same request control.
  • Do not make a second booking, payment or refund just because the first response is late.
  • If your role or branch changes, private data is cleared or reloaded. Reopen the record in the correct branch.
  • Read validation beside a field, correct the entry and review again. Never make up a reference to get past a form.
